My Daily Routine

Listening: put the sentences in order · Matching: join the sentence halves

Task 1. Put the sentences in order

Listen to the recording. Rearrange the sentences so that they follow the order in which you hear them. Drag the sentences, or use the arrow buttons to move them up and down.

Task 2. Match the sentence beginnings and endings

The beginnings are in the original order. Match each beginning with the correct ending. Drag an ending to a dotted space, or tap an ending and then tap the space where it belongs. You can change your answers at any time.
