Дні тижня та місяці

Put the words in order and practise spelling the months!

Task 1. Days of the week

Arrange the days in order, starting with понеділок and ending with неділя. The boxes are vertical. Drag them or use the arrows.

Task 2. Months of the year

Arrange the months in the exact order given: грудень, січень, лютий, березень … листопад. The boxes are vertical. Drag them or use the arrows.

Task 3. Unscramble the months

Make the names of the months by rearranging their letters. Tap a letter in the bank, then tap a box to place it. Tap a filled box to return its letter.
