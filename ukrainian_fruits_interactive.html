🍎 Ukrainian Fruits and Berries 🍇

Learn the Ukrainian names. Look, match, complete and spell!

Task 1. Match the words and pictures

Choose the correct Ukrainian word for each picture.

Task 2. Complete the sentences

Drag a word into a gap, or tap a word and then tap a gap. Use each word once.

Task 3. Look and type
